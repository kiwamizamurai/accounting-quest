# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part in this project without being harassed, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps make the community welcoming:

- Being kind and patient with people who are learning, including learning accounting.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its learners.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, whether public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Please report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@kiwamizamurai](https://github.com/kiwamizamurai), through the contact options on their GitHub profile. All reports will be reviewed and handled in confidence.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who behaves in a way that is harmful to the community.
